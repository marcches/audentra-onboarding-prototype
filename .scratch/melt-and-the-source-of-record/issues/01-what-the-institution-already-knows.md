# 01 — What the institution already knows

Type: research
Status: open

**The question:** How much of what the product asks an admitted student can be
answered from what the institution already holds — the application, the campus
visit record, the CRM — and what does the product do about the part that cannot?

This is the client's own framing and it is a commercial argument before it is a
design one:

> One of the things I keep hearing from schools is that they can't get admitted
> students to fill out their new student questionnaire. So they **melt** even
> before they started any of the process. […] I want to be able to speak to how
> we would address this if it comes up. Like schools can't get students to fill
> out their stuff — how would we get them to do ours?

Two answers were already offered in the same conversation and both need work
before they can be said out loud to a school:

- **Prefill.** The stated rule today is to gather from the university's own
  database whatever it has — name, last name, phone — and to make the onboarding
  shorter. What is missing is the honest inventory: which fields are genuinely
  available from a typical institution, which are available but stale, and which
  the student must answer no matter what. A claim of "we prefill it" that turns
  out to cover four fields of thirty is worse than not making it.
- **Gamification.** Offered as the other half, with physiological backing on
  incentivising and time-scaling to be brought by the designer. That backing
  changes *how much* a Quest should be worth and how fast **Decay** should run —
  it does not change where either lives.

**What this ticket is not.** It is not a design ticket and nothing is drawn here.
The visual grammar of a prefilled field is already being built in the current
cycle against fixtures, deliberately, so that it is not designed twice. What is
missing is the data reality underneath it and the argument that goes to a school.

**What would resolve it:** an inventory of fields against what a real institution
can supply, a position on stale data, and a written answer to the objection —
short enough to say in a meeting.

**Blocked by:** the designer's physiological research on incentives and time
scaling, which she is bringing. Not blocked by anything in this repository.

**Status:** open — needs the client and the designer in the conversation, not an
agent.

- [ ] An inventory exists of which fields an institution can actually supply
- [ ] A position exists on data that is available but out of date
- [ ] The fields the student must answer regardless are named
- [ ] The physiological backing on incentives and time scaling is attached
- [ ] A written answer to the melt objection exists, short enough to say aloud
- [ ] Whether any of it changes the Points values or the Decay rate is decided
