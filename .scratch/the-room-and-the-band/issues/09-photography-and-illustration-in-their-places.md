# 09 — Photography and illustration, each in its place

**What to build:** Imagery stops being incidental and gets a rule a student can
feel. **Photographs carry what is real** — the residences they are ranking, the
campus they are moving to, their own student card. **Illustration carries what is
abstract** — an empty state, an area that is not built yet, the moment Points are
awarded.

The division is not decorative. A student choosing where to live needs to judge a
room, and a drawing of a room cannot be judged. The apps that illustrate heavily
do so because they have nothing real to show; this product has residences, a
campus and a person, and the photography is information rather than atmosphere.

This is also what turns the seven unbuilt areas from looking broken into looking
finished-and-empty, and what gives the reward moment something to be other than a
number changing.
