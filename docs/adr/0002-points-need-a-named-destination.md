# Points need a named destination

Points were shown as a bare `+50` beside a completed rail item, which is a
receipt handed to someone who has already finished — it motivates nobody and
adds weight to the one component we were trying to lighten. Every mature rewards
UI we looked at on Mobbin (Everyday Rewards, Qantas, Ulta, Langdock) shows points
as **distance to a named thing**, never as a bare score, so Points here are
denominated in campus bookstore credit and always shown against that
destination.

## Considered Options

- **Named tiers instead of credit** (Bronze → Silver → Gold freshman). Rejected
  on social grounds, not design ones: it ranks a cohort against each other before
  any of them has arrived on campus, and the rank would be visible on a screen
  the student's family may be sitting next to them for.
- **A bare scoreboard.** Rejected: this is what exists today and is the thing
  being replaced.

## Consequences

- Points appear at the moment they are earned (a transition on Phase or Quest
  completion) and otherwise rest in a single **Balance**. They are never printed
  next to every rail item as a standing price list.
- The bookstore-credit conversion rate is a fixture in this prototype. It is
  presented as plausible, not authoritative, and the real rate must come from the
  institution before this ships to a student.
